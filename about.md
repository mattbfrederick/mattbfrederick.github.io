My name is Matt Frederick and I am a wireless enthusiast and a WLAN consultant.

I created this blog to do my part to help deepen the pool of knowledge available in the areas I work in.

I have been involved in wireless networking professionally since 2008. I have worked extensively in many areas, such as:

- Routing and switching
- Campus and enterprise WiFi
- Very High Density/Large Public Venue WiFi
- SCADA wireless
- PTP (licensed and unlicensed)
- PMP
- Mesh

I hold a number of certifications.

- CWNE \#197
- Ekahau Certified Master \#27
- ACMP (Aruba Certified Mobility Professional)
- CWDP (Certified Wireless Design Professional)
- CWAP (Certified Wireless Analysis Professional)
- CWSP (Certified Wireless Security Professional)
- CCNP (R/S) (Cisco Certified Network Professional)
- BCNP (Brocade Certified Network Professional)
- CCNA-Wireless (Cisco Certified Network Associate Wireless)
- CWNA (Certified Wireless Network Administrator)
- Completed certification courses for Firetide, Strix, Bridgewave, Xirrus, Ceragon
